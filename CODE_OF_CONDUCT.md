# Community Conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Critique proposals and code with concrete evidence.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse and disclosure of private information are unacceptable.
Do not post customer transcripts, credentials, or personal information in public discussions.

The maintainer may remove harmful content or restrict participation and should explain moderation decisions privately where practical.
Report community conduct concerns privately to [sealdot404@gmail.com](mailto:sealdot404@gmail.com).
The maintainer approved publication of this contact. Do not disclose reports or
personal details in public issues. No fixed response SLA is promised.
This document is a minimal project policy, not a claim of adoption of a third-party code of conduct.
